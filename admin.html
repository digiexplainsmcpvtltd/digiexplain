Formula methods

Area Rate × width × height (feet). For flex, vinyl and sign boards.

Per piece Rate × quantity. For fixed unit-price items where a reliable per-piece rate is entered.

Fixed job Enter the full job rate; quantity is ignored. For standees, roll-ups or custom jobs when the PDF gives a package price.

Tiered / manual Use a manually entered rate for the chosen quantity/variant. Visiting cards, envelopes and booklets may need different rates by quantity, GSM, sides, finish or size; create separate rate entries for each variant.

The source PDF has some ambiguous or repeated entries (for example, sign-board rates and some quantity tables). This tool does not silently resolve them; use the Note field to flag “Check source” and confirm your official selling price.
